Understanding Interoception

What Is Interoception?

Beyond the five senses lies interoception -- your body's internal awareness system. It is how you feel hunger, sense your heartbeat, and recognize emotions before they escalate.

The Science

Built on Research

InteroSense is grounded in peer-reviewed research and established clinical frameworks, bringing laboratory-validated methods to your daily practice.

MABT Methodology

Exercises are built on Mindfulness-Based Awareness Training, a clinically validated approach to developing interoceptive awareness through progressive body-based mindfulness.

Interoception Framework

Informed by Kelly Mahler's interoception curriculum, our approach systematically builds body awareness from foundational sensing to complex emotional integration.

Clinical Validation

Assessment tools use gold-standard validated scales. The neuroscience of interoception connects the insular cortex, vagus nerve, and autonomic regulation to mental health outcomes.
