Research

Built on Research

Every exercise and assessment in InteroSense is grounded in peer-reviewed neuroscience research.

MABT Methodology

Based on Mindful Awareness in Body-oriented Therapy, a clinically validated approach developed by Dr. Cynthia Price for teaching interoceptive awareness through progressive body-based exercises.

Interoception Framework

Built on the latest interoception research showing how body signal awareness directly influences emotional regulation, decision-making, and mental health outcomes across clinical populations.

Clinical Validation

Incorporates validated clinical instruments (GAD-7, PHQ-9, PCL-5) and evidence-based protocols used in clinical trials, ensuring professional-grade measurement and tracking.
