---
name: Body-pattern privacy boundary
description: Privacy and interpretation limits for body-pattern personalization.
---

Use structured Body Map regions, sensations, intensity, dates, and structured check-in sensations or areas for deterministic pattern summaries and recommendation reasons. Do not process free-text check-in or exercise notes for personalization unless the product later adds a clear, explicit opt-in.

**Why:** Free text can contain sensitive health details. Sending or analyzing it would expand the privacy scope, while structured fields already support useful descriptive personalization.

**How to apply:** Describe only recurrence, frequency, time window, and the user's own ratings. Do not infer medical causes or diagnoses from sensation location, intensity, wearable readings, or repeated entries.
