---
name: Health product claims standard
description: Durable product rule for scientific, clinical, wearable, and AI-generated claims.
---

Interosense should present itself as an evidence-informed wellness and reflection product, not as a diagnostic or clinical treatment service. User-facing content must not claim that a practice changed cortisol, vagal tone, brain structure, inflammation, HRV, or symptoms unless the product directly measured that outcome and the interpretation is validated.

**Why:** A product audit found that plausible mechanisms had been written as guaranteed personal outcomes. This weakened scientific credibility and created safety, privacy, and trust risks.

**How to apply:** Use bounded language, preserve uncertainty, tie evidence to specific interventions and outcomes, treat MAIA-2 as an eight-subscale profile, keep AI non-diagnostic, and never interpret a single wearable reading as health status.
