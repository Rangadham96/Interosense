---
name: Pathway safety adaptation
description: Durable product rules for progressing and adapting the focused 14-day body-signal pathway.
---

The 14-day pathway advances after a completed, saved practice with an acceptable rating and comfort response. A low rating or low comfort repeats the current day. Repeated low comfort switches to a gentler support practice before progression. Missed days do not reset progress or create catch-up work. Changes in sensation clarity are outcomes to observe, not gates for increasing or reducing difficulty.

**Why:** The pathway should adapt to the user's reported safety and comfort without rewarding streak pressure, treating greater sensation awareness as inherently better, or pushing through discomfort.

**How to apply:** Keep the sequence deterministic. Ask the same clarity and comfort questions before and after practice. Use only completion, rating, and comfort for progression decisions. Preserve each attempt for export and end-of-pathway comparison.
