---
name: Weekly useful-change review
description: Measurement rules for comparing body-signal practice outcomes across weeks.
---

Weekly review metrics must remain separate and traceable to direct user responses. Sensation clarity and comfort come from pathway responses, recovery after stress comes from its own check-in question, and helpfulness comes from exercise ratings. Missing values stay missing.

**Why:** Combining unlike scales or deriving recovery from stress, mood, or wearable data would create a result the user never reported and could make an observational product look clinical.

**How to apply:** Compare rolling seven-day periods for each metric independently. Use neutral higher, similar, and lower language. Never label a change as improvement, decline, treatment response, or proof of effectiveness.
