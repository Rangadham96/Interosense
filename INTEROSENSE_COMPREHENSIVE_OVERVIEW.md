## 1. Our Vision

**Interosense exists to give every person access to the one sense they've never been taught to use.**

Interoception — the brain's ability to sense and interpret signals from inside the body — is the hidden root of emotional intelligence, mental health, and self-understanding. It determines how accurately you detect your heartbeat, hunger, anxiety, joy, and pain. Research consistently shows that people with higher interoceptive awareness:

- Identify and regulate emotions more effectively
- Have significantly lower rates of anxiety and depression
- Make better intuitive decisions
- Recover from stress faster
- Experience higher overall wellbeing

Yet despite its profound importance, interoception is almost entirely absent from wellness education, therapy training, and mainstream mental health apps. Headspace teaches mindfulness. Calm teaches relaxation. **No one teaches the body.** Interosense is changing that.

### Our Mission
To make clinically-validated interoceptive training accessible, beautiful, and personalised to every individual — regardless of their condition, background, or experience level.

### Who We're For
- People with anxiety, depression, PTSD, panic disorder, chronic pain, eating disorders, or alexithymia
- High performers seeking emotional precision and stress resilience
- Clinicians looking for evidence-based self-practice tools for their clients
- Anyone curious about the science of inner experience

### Our Differentiators
- **Science-first, not spirituality-first.** Every exercise is grounded in peer-reviewed research. We cite the exact studies.
- **Condition-aware personalisation.** The app knows your conditions and tailors every recommendation accordingly.
- **Clinical-grade assessment.** We use MAIA-2 (37 items, 8 dimensions) — the gold standard validated instrument — to measure body awareness.
- **AI-powered daily insight.** Sense, our AI advisor (powered by Claude), delivers personalised daily insights based on your history, conditions, check-in data, and practice patterns.
- **No spirituality tax.** No mantras, crystals, or chakras. Pure science, pure practice.

## 2. The Science We're Built On

### The Insula — Your Inner Brain
The insular cortex, tucked deep within the brain, is interoception's command centre. It receives signals from every internal organ and creates a unified "body map." People who practise interoception develop a measurably thicker insular cortex with more precise and accurate internal awareness. Every exercise in Interosense is designed to stimulate and strengthen insular cortex activation.

### MABT — Our Core Methodology
The majority of our exercises are grounded in **Mindful Awareness in Body-oriented Therapy (MABT)**, developed by Cynthia Price, PhD, at the University of Washington. MABT uses a three-step framework that structures every exercise in the app:

- **Notice:** Detect a body sensation. Where is it? Does anything exist in this area?
- **Describe:** Articulate its qualities. Warm, pulsing, tight, cool, heavy, buzzing.
- **Connect:** Link the sensation to an emotional or psychological meaning. What is this sensation telling you?

This framework is evidence-based, with clinical trials demonstrating reduced PTSD symptoms, improved emotional regulation, and decreased substance use relapse rates.

### The Research Underpinning Every Feature

| Feature | Research Basis |
|---|---|
| Heartbeat detection exercises | Garfinkel et al., 2015 — Biological Psychology |
| Breathing/vagal activation | Hopper et al., 2019 — Journal of Clinical Nursing |
| Body scanning | Price & Hooven, 2018 — Frontiers in Psychology |
| MAIA-2 assessment | Mehling et al., 2018 — PLOS ONE |
| Anxiety reduction | Paulus & Stein, 2010 — Brain Structure and Function |
| Chronic pain | Zeidan et al., 2015 — Journal of Neuroscience |
| PTSD & trauma | van der Kolk, 2014; Porges, 2011 |
| Gut-brain axis | 95% serotonin in gut; vagus nerve research |
| HRV & nervous system | Critchley et al., 2004 — Nature Neuroscience |
| Emotional granularity | Herbert et al., 2011 — PLoS ONE |

### The Autonomic Nervous System
Interosense teaches users to navigate their own autonomic nervous system — the system governing heart rate, digestion, breathing, and stress response — through direct interoceptive practice. We explicitly teach:

- **Sympathetic activation** (fight/flight): what it feels like, how to recognise it early, how to interrupt it
- **Parasympathetic activation** (rest/digest): how to induce it through breath, body awareness, and temperature
- **Polyvagal theory** (Porges): ventral vagal (safe/social), sympathetic (mobilised), dorsal vagal (shutdown/freeze)

## 4. Technical Architecture

### Stack Overview

```
Frontend:   Expo React Native (SDK 54)
            → React Native Web (browser)
            → Expo Go (iOS/Android mobile)

Backend:    Express.js + TypeScript
            → Port 5000
            → Serves REST API + static web build + landing page

Database:   PostgreSQL (primary, cloud-synced)
            + AsyncStorage (offline cache / fallback)

AI:         Anthropic Claude API (claude-3-5-haiku)
            → Daily personalised insights
            → Once-per-day caching per user

Auth:       Express-session + bcrypt
            → Cookie-based sessions
            → PostgreSQL session store (connect-pg-simple)

ORM:        Drizzle ORM + drizzle-kit
Schema:     TypeScript-first, shared between frontend and backend

State:      React Context (AppContext + AuthContext)
            + React Query (@tanstack/react-query)
            + Bidirectional cloud sync on login
```

### Routing Architecture
```
/ → Landing page (HTML)
/app → Static Expo web build (baseUrl="/app")
/pitch → Investor pitch deck (HTML)
/api/* → REST API endpoints
/assets → Static asset serving
```

### Bidirectional Cloud Sync
- **On exercise completion:** Written to both PostgreSQL (server/routes.ts) and AsyncStorage
- **On check-in:** Written to both PostgreSQL and AsyncStorage
- **On login:** Server hydrates AppContext — fetches all sessions, checkins, assessments, loads into React state
- **On logout:** AsyncStorage cleared, AppContext reset
- **Offline:** AsyncStorage serves as complete local cache

## 5. Onboarding Flow (7 Steps)

The onboarding is the first impression of Interosense's science-first character. It is not a marketing funnel — it is a clinical intake, designed with the same care you'd find in a psychological assessment.

**Visual design:** Light purple-tinted gradient background. Large step progress indicator at the top. Each step slides in from the right. The "Next" button is a full-width purple rounded pill. Science copy everywhere.

### Step 1 — Safety Check
**What it does:** Asks the user to rate their current emotional state on a 1-10 scale. Framed as "Where are you right now?" with a slider.

**Why:** This is a clinical safety screen. If a user scores ≤ 2, they are immediately redirected to the Crisis Toolkit with supportive, non-alarming copy: "It sounds like you might need some immediate support. Let us help right now." This prevents the app from being used as a coping mechanism during acute crisis without safety resources being offered first.

**Copy:** Warm, clinical, non-judgmental. "This helps us tailor everything to where you are right now."

### Step 2 — Condition Selection
**What it does:** Presents 7 condition cards in a scrollable grid. Users select all that apply. Each card has the condition name, a colour-coded icon, and a one-line description.

**Conditions offered:** Anxiety, Panic Disorder, PTSD & Trauma, Depression, Eating Disorders, Chronic Pain, Alexithymia.

**Why:** The entire app — exercise recommendations, AI insights, clinical assessments, the 4-week programme — is personalised based on these selections. This is the single most important personalisation data point.

**Science note shown:** "Interoception research shows that body-brain training is effective for all of these conditions. You're in the right place."

### Step 3 — Body Awareness Baseline
**What it does:** A 5-question adaptive quiz measuring the user's starting interoceptive awareness. Questions like "How well can you detect your heartbeat without touching your chest?" (scored 1-5). Responses are stored as `interoceptiveBaseline` on the user profile.

**Why:** Establishes the starting point for the MAIA-2 trajectory. Lets the app calibrate exercise difficulty (beginners get beginner exercises first). Creates a meaningful "before" measurement.

**Visual design:** One question at a time. Five clean radio buttons per question. Progress bar. Calming purple-white gradient.

### Step 4 — "Aha Moment" Science Screen
**What it does:** A full-screen infographic-style explainer of interoception — what it is, why it matters for the user's specific selected conditions, and the neuroscience behind why this works.

**Why:** This is the most distinctive screen in the app. Instead of moving straight to "let's set your goals," we stop and educate. This builds trust, increases motivation, and frames everything that follows as meaningful rather than arbitrary.

**Content adapts to conditions.** If a user selected Anxiety, they see: "Your amygdala fires a false alarm when it misinterprets body signals. Interoceptive training closes the accuracy gap." If they selected PTSD: "Van der Kolk showed that trauma is stored in the body. Reclaiming body awareness is the path to healing."

### Step 5 — Experience Level
**What it does:** Three clear options presented as cards: Beginner (new to body awareness practices), Intermediate (some experience with mindfulness/body work), Advanced (experienced practitioner).

**Why:** Sets the default exercise difficulty filter, the pace of the 4-week programme, and the complexity of the AI advisor language.

### Step 6 — Daily Practice Goal
**What it does:** A time picker (5 / 10 / 15 / 20+ minutes per day). Presented with realistic expectation-setting: "Even 5 minutes daily produces measurable neural change within 3 weeks."

**Why:** Prevents the most common wellness app failure mode — over-ambitious commitment followed by abandonment. Research on habit formation shows small, consistent practice beats sporadic intense sessions.

### Step 7 — Your 4-Week Programme
**What it does:** Generates and displays a personalised 4-week programme based on all previous inputs. Each week has a theme and a list of exercises, with reasoning for each recommendation.

**Week 1 example (Anxiety, Beginner):**
- Week 1 Theme: "Building Your Foundation" — Box Breathing, Heartbeat Detection, Progressive Body Scan
- Week 2 Theme: "Deepening Awareness" — Diaphragmatic Breathing, Focused Body Scan, Temperature Sensing
- Week 3 Theme: "Connecting Body to Emotion" — 4-7-8 Breathing, Heartbeat & Emotion Link, Tension Release
- Week 4 Theme: "Integration & Expansion" — Full Body Scan, Exposure Introduction, Gut Signal Awareness

The user sees this programme in their Profile tab permanently. It feels like having a personalised therapist-designed curriculum.

**Final action:** Account creation (if not already registered). The flow transitions seamlessly to the main app.

## 8. Exercise Player — The Full Journey

This is the centrepiece of the app. The exercise experience is designed to feel as good as a premium guided meditation but grounded in clinical precision.

### Phase 1 — Pre-Start Science Screen

Before starting, the user sees a full-screen information card containing:

**Header:** Exercise title + category colour badge + duration

**Science Note** (displayed in a teal-bordered card):
The research behind this exercise. Written in accessible language but with full academic grounding. Example for Heartbeat Detection:
> "Heartbeat detection tasks are the gold standard in interoception research. The insular cortex processes cardiac signals, and training this pathway strengthens the neural bridge between body and conscious awareness."

**Benefits** (bullet list with checkmarks in purple):
Each benefit is specific and evidence-tagged. Example:
- Improves cardiac interoceptive accuracy
- Reduces anxiety sensitivity by 23% (Garfinkel et al., 2015)
- Grounds attention in present-moment body signals
- Builds foundation for emotional awareness

**Preparation Tips** (amber info card):
Practical preparation instructions. Example:
- Find a quiet space free from distractions
- Sit or lie in a comfortable position
- Avoid caffeine 30 minutes before practice

**Expected Sensations** (purple tinted card):
What the user will actually feel. Sets accurate expectations and reduces anxiety about unusual sensations. Example:
- Subtle pulsing in chest or throat
- Tingling in fingertips
- Warmth spreading through limbs
- Heightened awareness of breathing

**Contraindication Warning** (amber banner — only if applicable):
Displayed in a warm amber card with a warning icon for any contraindications relevant to the user's conditions.

**Research Citation** (small grey text at bottom):
The full citation for the primary research study. Example:
> Garfinkel et al. (2015). Knowing your own heart: Distinguishing interoceptive accuracy from interoceptive sensibility. Biological Psychology, 104, 65-74.

**CTA Button:** "Begin Exercise →" (full-width purple rounded button)

**Safety Escape:** A small "I need help" link at the bottom → Crisis Toolkit

**Layout:**
- **Step counter:** "Step 3 of 9" in small teal text

## 9. Tab 3 — Daily Check-In

**Colour scheme:** Warm gradient from teal-to-purple at the top. White cards below. Progress bar in purple.

**Philosophy:** The check-in is not a chore. It's a daily practice in itself — a moment of structured self-reflection that builds the habit of internal listening.

- "Your Daily Body Check" heading
- Time estimate: "About 3 minutes"
- Brief science note: "Research shows that daily body awareness check-ins accelerate interoceptive development by creating consistent attention habits."
- Large "Begin Check-In" purple button

**Prompt:** "How connected to your body do you feel right now?"
**Input:** Large interactive slider (1-10), with labels: 1 = "Completely disconnected" / 10 = "Fully present"
**Science context:** Small note beneath: "This measures your baseline interoceptive availability for today."

**Input:** 1-10 slider with emoji-free descriptors: 1 = "Exhausted" / 10 = "Vibrant"

**Prompt:** "If you had to name your dominant mood right now, what is it?"

## 14. Clinical Assessments

**Purpose:** Validated psychological assessment instruments that provide clinically meaningful data and track mental health trends over time.

### Available Assessments

**GAD-7 (Generalised Anxiety Disorder Scale)**
- 7 items, scored 0-3 (Not at all → Nearly every day)
- Total score 0-21
- Severity thresholds: Minimal (0-4), Mild (5-9), Moderate (10-14), Severe (15-21)
- Validated for GAD screening in primary care (Spitzer et al., 2006)
- Recommended for: Anxiety, Panic Disorder users

**PHQ-9 (Patient Health Questionnaire)**
- 9 items, scored 0-3
- Total score 0-27
- Severity thresholds: Minimal (0-4), Mild (5-9), Moderate (10-14), Moderately Severe (15-19), Severe (20-27)
- Most widely used depression screening tool globally (Kroenke et al., 2001)
- Recommended for: Depression users

**PCL-5 (PTSD Checklist)**
- 20 items, scored 0-4
- Total score 0-80
- Threshold score 33 suggests PTSD diagnosis warranting clinical assessment
- Validated against DSM-5 PTSD criteria (Weathers et al., 2013)
- Recommended for: PTSD & Trauma users

### Assessment Flow
1. **Intro screen** — Assessment name, purpose, time estimate, validation note
2. **Question screen** — One question at a time. Large readable text. Tappable answer options (highlighted on selection). Progress bar.
3. **Results screen** — Score prominently displayed. Severity label with explanation. What it means for this individual. Clinical interpretation. Next steps (continue practice, consider professional support). Saved to profile.

All assessment results are stored in the `assessments` table and appear in the Progress tab's clinical history.

## 15. MAIA-2 Body Awareness Assessment

The MAIA-2 is the most sophisticated feature of the Interosense platform — and what makes it unique among all mental wellness apps.

**What it is:** The Multidimensional Assessment of Interoceptive Awareness, 2nd edition. Developed by Wolf Mehling, MD, at the University of California San Francisco. Published in PLOS ONE (2018). The gold standard validated instrument for measuring interoceptive awareness in research and clinical settings.

**37 items across 8 dimensions.** Rated on a 6-point Likert scale (0 = Never, 5 = Always). Some items are reverse-scored. Takes approximately 10-15 minutes.

### The 8 MAIA-2 Subscales

| Subscale | Items | What It Measures |
|---|---|---|
| Noticing | 4 | Awareness of comfortable, uncomfortable, and neutral body sensations |
| Not-Distracting | 3 | Not ignoring or distracting from pain/discomfort |
| Not-Worrying | 3 | Not worrying or experiencing distress about sensations |
| Attention Regulation | 7 | Sustaining and controlling attention to body sensations |
| Emotional Awareness | 5 | Connection between body sensations and emotional states |
| Self-Regulation | 4 | Regulating distress by attending to body sensations |
| Body Listening | 3 | Active listening to body for emotional insight |
| Trusting | 3 | Experience of body as safe and trustworthy |

### Assessment Screen Design
- White background. One question per screen with smooth slide transitions.
- Question text in Nunito 600 (18px)
- 6 labelled response buttons in a vertical stack
- Current subscale name shown subtly at top
- Progress: "Question 12 of 37"
- Back button to revise previous answers

### Results Screen

## 18. The Sense AI Advisor

**What it is:** Interosense's AI-powered daily insight engine. Named "Sense" — a deliberate reference to both sensory awareness and good judgment.

**Powered by:** Anthropic Claude API (claude-3-5-haiku). The model is carefully prompted to act as an interoception coach, not a generic wellness chatbot.

### How It Works

**The prompt context includes:**
- User's name and conditions (e.g., "Sarah has GAD and PTSD")
- Recent check-in data (awareness score, stress, sleep, energy, mood, sensations)
- Recent exercise history (last 5-7 sessions with ratings)
- Time of day (morning / afternoon / evening)
- Day of week and practice streak
- User experience level

**The prompt instructs Claude to:**
- Deliver a 2-3 sentence personalised daily insight rooted in interoceptive science
- Reference the user's specific body signals or recent practice
- Offer one concrete micro-practice suggestion
- Use warm, non-patronising language
- Never give medical advice
- Never use spiritual or religious language

**Example output (high stress, low sleep, completed box breathing yesterday):**
> "Your check-in suggests your nervous system is working overtime today — stress up, sleep down. Yesterday's box breathing session gave your vagus nerve a workout. This morning, even 4 cycles before your first meeting could meaningfully lower your cortisol before it builds."

### Caching
Each user receives exactly one AI-generated insight per day. The insight is cached in the `daily_insights` table. Subsequent page loads retrieve the cached version. This prevents excessive API costs and ensures consistency throughout the day.

### Fallback
If the Anthropic API key is unavailable, the app falls back seamlessly to one of 32 hand-written science-backed insight cards. These are high-quality, condition-aware static insights that maintain the premium experience.

### The 9 Personalisation Factors (Rule Engine)
Separately from the AI, a deterministic rule engine (lib/personalization-engine.ts) analyses 9+ factors to:
1. Recommend the next exercise (shown on the Home screen)
2. Personalise the check-in completion message
3. Flag when rest days are needed
4. Surface condition-specific exercises after difficult check-ins

The factors analysed:
1. User conditions
2. Time of day (morning = energising, evening = calming)
3. Recent exercise history (prevents repetition)
4. Average check-in scores for the last 7 days
5. Current stress level
6. Current energy level
7. Sleep quality
8. Experience level
9. Exercises not yet tried (encourages exploration)

- No third-party analytics SDK installed
- No advertising infrastructure
- No data sold to third parties (promised in onboarding trust card)

- GDPR-compliant architecture (right to erasure, data portability)

- Express sessions with `httpOnly: true` cookies
- CORS configured specifically for Replit dev and production domains
- `bcrypt` with 12 salt rounds — industry standard
- Zod schema validation on all API inputs
- SQL injection protection via Drizzle ORM parameterised queries
